About TerraNova Engineering Lab

TerraNova is an independent engineering lab founded by Paula Cavalcanti. We coach competitive student teams and individual builders across US (TARC, RWDC) and European (CanSat, Jugend forscht) aerospace tracks.

Looking for a competition mentor? We are actively interested in advising and mentoring student teams for the upcoming European aerospace competition cycle—including German / European CanSat and Jugend forscht (Technik).
