Mentorship Approach // Ground Zero Design

At TerraNova Engineering Lab, projects are taken completely from ground zero for middle and high school students. While working with all skill levels and abilities, the focus is on high-challenge aerospace endeavors—where students often start with zero prior experience in drones or complex design. Even with experience, every team designs from scratch.

Diverse skill levels are blended by strengthening traditional teaching methods, incorporating smart research workflows, and maintaining complete transparency (including full disclosures regarding tool utilization where applicable).

Initial foundational development and club mentorship took place at Milford High School (MHS) by building these programs from the ground up and acquiring funding for a Radio Frequency Station. Advanced rocket design and subsequent TARC milestones were later formalized through Terranova Engineering Lab, a grassroots engineering initiative.
